Purpose
The purpose of this Change Request (CR) is to introduce a configurable Ceiling Increase Percentage for Repeat Loans within BRAC’s ERP
system to ensure accurate, automated, and policy-compliant loan approval for repeat borrowers. Currently, repeat loan approvals follow
the existing role-wise approver limits without a configurable percentage-based condition, which may not adequately reflect increased risk
or required oversight when loan amounts grow significantly.
This enhancement will allow authorized users to define, edit, or omit an increased percentage threshold for repeat loans (specific to Progoti
and ADP projects). Based on this threshold, the system will automatically determine and assign the appropriate next-level approver when a
repeat loan proposal exceeds or meets the defined percentage. This will strengthen governance, improve consistency in loan approvals,
and reduce manual intervention while ensuring adherence to hierarchical approval policies.
Users
Primary users include Super Admins (SA) and Microfinance Programme Administrators (MF_Prog_Admin), who will configure the increase
percentage in the Loan Policy setup.
Secondary users include loan approvers involved in the approval workflow: BM, AM, AAM, RM, SRM, SPM, PM, DPH, AD, and HO, who will
see and act on system-assigned approvers during loan approval.
Scope of this CR
This section provides a clear outline of what falls within the scope of the proposed changes and what is excluded from consideration.
Scope Identification Description
• Addition of Increased Condition for Repeat Loans (%) field in Role Wise Approver Limit
for Progoti and ADP projects.
• Ability for SA and MF_Prog_Admin to add, edit, or omit the percentage (0 = not
applicable).
• Default value set to 40%, numeric-only, non-mandatory.
In Scope • Display of Repeat Loan Percentage and Repeat Loan Approver in the approver limit grid.
• Automatic calculation of repeat loan percentage based on the member’s latest two loans
(current or closed).
• Automated selection of the next-level approver when the proposed amount meets or
exceeds the defined percentage.
• Automatic application of this logic in the Loan Approval UI.
• Projects other than Progoti and ADP.
• Loan Modification, Loan Refinancing, Loan Rescheduling, UPG Loan Merge
• Changes to approve hierarchy structures.
Out of Scope
• New loan products or approval roles.
• ERP workflow or data entry changes unrelated to repeat loan approval logic.
• Integration with external systems or tools.
